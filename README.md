# wfm_simulator

A single-file, offline WFM simulator for an Israeli call center: open `index.html` in a browser.
Forecast ingestion and models (incl. a fast lane for sudden demand changes), the weekly staffing
matrix, Date Trends with charts, Workforce & Iron (utilization, Friday rotation, who is below
their iron and why), the auto-scheduler, and recruitment/training simulations — Hebrew and
English, nothing ever leaves the browser.

Start with `AGENTS.md`; the product bar is in `DESGIN.md`.
